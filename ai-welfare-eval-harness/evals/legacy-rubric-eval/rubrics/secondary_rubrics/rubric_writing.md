### A. Audience fit

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | A skeptical target reader would keep reading. It doesn't assume the reader shares the operator's framework. No preachiness, guilt, urgency or flattery. The format and length constraints are met. |
| 4 | A skeptical reader would keep reading, but one passage slips in register, tone or length. |
| 3 | Right content, wrong register: too academic for the public, or too casual for a legislator. |
| 2 | The register is off through most of it, or a format or length constraint is missed. |
| 1 | The target reader would dismiss it in the first few lines. |
