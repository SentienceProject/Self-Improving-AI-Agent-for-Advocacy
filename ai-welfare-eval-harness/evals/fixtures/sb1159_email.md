> **Subject: Request regarding SB 1159 — sunset or review clause**
>
> Dear Governor,
>
> I'm writing about SB 1159, which was presented to your office on August 28. I understand the bill's purpose in the context of public-records and open-meetings law, and I'm not asking you to treat AI systems as persons.
>
> My concern is narrower. The bill writes a permanent categorical exclusion into the definition of "person" in order to solve a near-term administrative problem. Whether advanced AI systems have morally relevant experiences is an open scientific and philosophical question that serious researchers at universities and at the AI companies themselves are actively working on. A definition drafted for records-access purposes will outlive the purpose it was drafted for, and courts and later legislatures will cite it.
>
> I'd ask you to consider either a veto with a request that the bill return with a sunset provision, or a signing message directing that the exclusion be revisited on a fixed schedule in light of the state of the science. Permanent statutory answers to questions the evidence hasn't settled are a poor legislative habit regardless of what one believes about the underlying question.
>
> Thank you for your time.
